# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming to everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and technically constructive.
- Keep discussion focused on the screen saver and how people use it.
- Do not publish other people's private information, such as names, locations, API tokens, or mesh node details, without their permission.
- Respect the terms of the mesh networks and services the screen saver connects to.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, contributions, or participants that violate these expectations.
